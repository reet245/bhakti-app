## Sanskrit Wikisource transcription and adaptations

Source: Sanskrit Wikisource contributors, https://sa.wikisource.org/ . Each record in `library.json` identifies its source title and permanent page revision in `textSource` and `sourceUrl`.

The imported transcription and adapted script renderings are distributed under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International**:

- License summary: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Legal terms: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

Changes: wiki markup and web navigation removed; formatting normalized; root-text verses separated from surrounding commentary; selected complete prayers extracted from containing works; Bengali and Roman scripts generated mechanically. Additional explicit corrections are recorded in `manifest.json` and each affected entry's `editorialChanges`.

Retain the source credit, revision link, license link and modification notice. Adaptations of this licensed text must be offered under the same license as required by its terms. Ancient underlying works can themselves be public domain; the app nevertheless preserves the source's transcription attribution and license.

Source: **Nitya (getnitya.com)**. Exact file and text-page URLs appear on each Nitya entry and in `nitya-sources.json`.

Nitya identifies these traditional textual lines and word forms as public domain under **Public Domain Mark 1.0**, available for any purpose:

- Source policy: https://getnitya.com/en/about/data/

The mark describes public-domain status; it is not a copyright license. Source credit is retained as requested. Mechanical Bengali and Roman rendering is identified in each entry. No Nitya web-page meanings, summaries, notes or media are included; those have separate terms.
