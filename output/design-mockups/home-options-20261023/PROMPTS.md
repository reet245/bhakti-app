A warm ivory background with extremely subtle handmade paper grain, deep cocoa text, terracotta/saffron accents and fine antique gold strokes. At top, an airy header. Below, a short wide panoramic devotional illustration recreating the six deities from the reference arranged around a subtle central Om, all inside a delicate scalloped Indian temple arch, occupying only the upper quarter, gently fading into the ivory canvas. The visual should feel calm, welcoming, practical, modern Indian craft, with generous breathing room. No photographic wallpaper behind text.
